## Shared form structure

Both pages use the Batanes State College Procurement Unit Supplier Evaluation Form (Goods) layout, the same confidentiality instruction, four rating columns, additional-comments area, and respondent name/signature plus date sign-off.

## End-User page

The End-User page is headed “To be accomplished by end-user” and records supplier name, type of goods/services provided, office/unit, and purchase-order number. It has nine required rating prompts:

1. Products/services met quality standards.
2. Products/services followed the specified delivery term.
3. Products/services were accurate and complete to specifications.
4. Supplier responded to inquiries and information requests.
5. Supplier addressed issues and provided solutions.
6. Supplier communicated clearly throughout ordering.
7. Supplier offered competitive pricing.
8. Products/services justified the cost.
9. Respondent would recommend the supplier within the institution.

## Procurement Office page

The Procurement Office page is headed “To be accomplished by Procurement Office” and records supplier name, purchase-request number, purchase-order number, supplier registry reference, registration date, and expiration date. It has five required rating prompts:

1. Supplier responded to the RFQ within the specified date.
2. Supplier offered competitive pricing against other suppliers/bidders.
3. Offer conformed to sample/specification requirements.
4. Supplier submitted required documentary requirements within 1–2 days upon Procurement Officer coordination.
5. Supplier delivered goods within the Purchase Order/contract term.

The original source labels one header field with a third-party registry acronym. ProcureWise preserves the field’s function under neutral “Supplier registry reference” terminology, consistent with the project’s existing neutral-catalog terminology requirement.
